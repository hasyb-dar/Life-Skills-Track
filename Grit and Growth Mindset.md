# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

### Question 1

Grit is the passion and perseverance to pursue long-term goals, even when the journey gets tough. Success depends not only on talent or intelligence, but also on hard work, patience and consistency.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

### Question 2

A growth mindset is the belief that our skills and abilities can be developed through learning, practice and effort. Rather than fearing mistakes, we should embrace challenges and treat them as chances to learn.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### Question 3

Internal Locus of Control is the belief that our own actions, decisions and efforts shape our results.
The key message of the video is to stop blaming circumstances or other people, focus on what we can control, take responsibility for our progress, and keep looking ahead to what comes next.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 4

Key points for building a growth mindset:

1. Trust in your ability to learn new things.
2. Welcome challenges and difficulties with a positive attitude.
3. Treat mistakes and failures as lessons.
4. Stay open to feedback from everyone.
5. Aim for improvement, not perfection.
6. Be patient and consistent.
7. Step outside your comfort zone and push yourself further.
8. Keep learning, always.
9. Believe that you can figure things out.
10. Value the learning process and enjoy it.

## 5. Mindset - A MountBlue Warrior Reference Manual

### Question 5

My ideas for building a growth mindset as a software developer:

1. Code every day and take on new problems.
2. Keep going when I get stuck on a tough problem.
3. Learn from my mistakes so I don't repeat them.
4. Accept feedback from mentors and teammates with a positive attitude.
5. Understand concepts deeply instead of memorizing code.
6. Explore new technologies and keep sharpening my technical skills.
7. Ask questions whenever something is unclear.
8. Learn from other developers and study their approaches.
9. Own my tasks and my mistakes.
10. Set small daily goals and track my progress.
11. Work on my weak areas instead of avoiding them.
12. Stay consistent and keep learning.

I believe that becoming a good software developer takes time, practice and patience. I will focus on improving a little every day instead of expecting instant results.
