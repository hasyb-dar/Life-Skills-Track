# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment can include any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature, whether verbal, visual, or physical. Examples include:

- **Verbal:** Inappropriate comments about someone's appearance or body, sexual jokes, suggestive remarks, offensive language, or repeatedly asking someone for dates or sexual favours.
- **Visual:** Sharing or displaying sexually explicit images, videos, posters, cartoons, emails, or messages.
- **Physical:** Unwanted touching, hugging, kissing, inappropriate gestures, blocking someone's movement, or invading their personal space.
- **Quid Pro Quo:** Offering promotions, rewards, or workplace benefits in exchange for sexual favours, or threatening someone's job for refusing such advances.
- **Hostile Work Environment:** Behaviour, comments, or actions that create an intimidating, offensive, or uncomfortable workplace.

## 2. What would you do if you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or witness sexual harassment, I would take the following steps:

1. **Address the behaviour:** If I feel safe and comfortable, I would clearly communicate that the behaviour is inappropriate and must stop.
2. **Document the incident:** I would maintain a record of what happened, including dates, times, locations, messages, and witnesses, if any.
3. **Report the incident:** I would report it to the company's Internal Committee (IC), HR department, or an appropriate senior authority, following the official grievance procedure.
4. **Support the affected person:** If I witness harassment, I would support the person affected, listen without judging them, and encourage them to report the incident.
5. **Maintain confidentiality:** I would respect everyone's privacy and avoid spreading information about the incident.
6. **Cooperate with the process:** I would cooperate with any investigation and follow the appropriate reporting procedure.

I believe everyone deserves a safe, respectful, and inclusive workplace. We all have a responsibility to prevent sexual harassment rather than ignore it.
